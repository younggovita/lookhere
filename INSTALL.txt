LOOKHERE
Show your screen, say what's wrong, paste it into any AI chat.

Lookhere records your screen and your voice while you point at things. When you stop,
the video is copied so you can paste it straight into ChatGPT, Claude or any other chat.


INSTALL
1. Double-click the Lookhere-Setup file (Lookhere-Setup-<version>.exe).
2. Windows may say "Windows protected your PC". This happens because the app isn't
   code-signed. Click "More info", then "Run anyway". It only asks once.
3. Follow the installer. "Start Lookhere when Windows starts" is ticked so it's always ready;
   untick it if you'd rather start it yourself.
4. The first time it opens, Lookhere downloads its speech model (about 460 MB). A small
   bar at the top of the screen shows this. It only happens once.

You need Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit). A microphone is what makes it useful, but without one
Lookhere still records the screen.


HOW TO USE IT
1. Press Ctrl+Alt+H to start recording. A small bar appears at the top of your screen,
   and a soft ding plays when it's recording.
2. Talk through your problem while you click around and point at things.
3. Press Ctrl+Alt+H again (or the red square on the bar) to finish.
4. A couple of seconds later the video is on your clipboard. Click into your AI chat
   and press Ctrl+V.

While recording:
  Ctrl+Alt+P   pause and resume (paused parts are cut out of the video you paste)
  Ctrl+Alt+M   draw on the screen (or click the pencil on the bar), drag to draw
  Esc          cancel and throw the recording away


THE TRAY ICON
Lookhere lives in the system tray (bottom right, you may need to click the ^ arrow).
  Left-click:   start or finish a recording
  Right-click:  the menu, with History (play, copy, trim or delete past recordings)
                and Settings (microphone, theme, shortcuts, start with Windows,
                recordings folder)


GOOD TO KNOW
- Your recordings stay on your PC. Nothing is uploaded unless you paste it somewhere.
- Lookhere only goes online to check for a new version (a few times a day) and, once, to
  download its speech model. No accounts, no tracking.
- Anything on your screen while recording is in the video, so close private stuff first.
- It understands spoken English only for now. The video works in any language.
- Recordings older than 30 days are deleted automatically (and for good).
- If your voice isn't picked up: right-click the tray icon > Settings > Microphone > Test.
  Also check Windows Settings > Privacy & security > Microphone lets desktop apps use it.
- To uninstall, use Windows Settings > Apps > Installed apps > Lookhere. Your recordings
  and settings are kept: to remove them too, delete the folder
  %LOCALAPPDATA%\Lookhere (paste that into File Explorer's address bar).
- Something not working? Tell us at https://github.com/younggovita/lookhere/issues
- Licences for the parts Lookhere is built on are in Start menu > Lookhere licences.


ON A MAC
You need a Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or later) running macOS 15 or later. A microphone
is what makes it useful, but Lookhere records the screen without one.

1. Open the Lookhere .dmg file (Lookhere-<version>-mac.dmg) and drag Lookhere into the
   Applications folder.
2. Open Lookhere from Applications. macOS checks it (it's signed and notarised by Apple)
   and asks whether to open an app downloaded from the internet. Click Open.
3. Lookhere lives in the menu bar (top right). The first time you record, macOS asks to
   let Lookhere use the microphone (click Allow) and record the screen (open System
   Settings and switch Lookhere on under Screen & System Audio Recording). Then quit
   Lookhere from its menu and open it again. Updates keep these permissions.
4. The first time it opens, Lookhere downloads its speech model (about 460 MB). It only
   happens once.

On a Mac the shortcuts use Ctrl+Option instead of Ctrl+Alt (Ctrl+Option+H to record),
and you paste with Cmd+V. When an update is out, click the menu bar icon, then "Update to".
To uninstall, switch off "Open at login" in Lookhere's Settings, quit Lookhere and drag it
from Applications to the Bin. Recordings and settings stay in
~/Library/Application Support/Lookhere until you delete that folder.
